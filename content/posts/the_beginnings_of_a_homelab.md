What is a homelab? There may well be many definitions bot for our purposes I'm going with a homelab being some form of server at home that you can work with, practise your server admin with and maybe even host live applications on. 

That being said: there were a few more things that I wanted from my own homelab:

## Automation

This thing has to be automated from start to finish. I'm a great believer in treating my servers like cattle and have no interest whatsoever in having manual setups in documents that I won't keep up to date and rely on a number of magic incantations. You know that this will go wrong, and I know that too - there's no point me trying to pretend that I'll remember everything when I change my hardware, when something breaks or whatever.

Fortunately for us there are a few pieces of software that will come in handy here, they will make sure that we have a setup that we can replicate (yeah - my partner might benefit from this to, this isn't just me using the royal we here, don't worry)
